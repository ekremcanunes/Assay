## Brand & Style

This design system translates the calculated restraint and high-density sophistication of elite execution desks into an architectural, daylight-optimized environment. It rejects clinical sterility in favor of crystalline optical clarity—marrying pure titanium whites, soft porcelain tones, and hyper-legible carbon typography with calibrated flashes of cobalt and emerald.

### Philosophy & Visual Movement
- **Movement:** High-Density Precision Modernism fused with Contemporary Swiss Structuralism.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, razor-sharp, analytical, and luminous.
- **Target Audience:** Institutional traders, portfolio managers, quantitative analysts, and financial engineers who require continuous readability across multi-hour analytical workflows without visual fatigue.
- **Atmosphere:** A pristine glass-and-steel trading floor flooded with controlled morning daylight. Every pixel occupies a deliberate coordinate; data density is elevated through crisp structural lines rather than visual clutter.

## Colors

The daylight palette balances high-albedo porcelain substrates against deep slate neutrals, accented with targeted telemetry hues that guide the eye without cognitive overload.

### Color Tiers
- **Substrate & Surfaces:**
  - `surface-base` (`#f8fafc`): The structural porcelain backing canvas.
  - `surface-card` (`#ffffff`): Pure crisp white for elevated interactive tiles, tables, and execution panels.
  - `surface-recessed` (`#f1f5f9`): Soft cool titanium for search rails, inactive tabs, and segmented control wells.
  - `border-hairline` (`#e2e8f0`): Ultra-sharp cool zinc boundary line.
  - `border-subtle` (`#cbd5e1`): Deliberate divider for dense data tables and split panes.

- **Content & Typography:**
  - `text-primary` (`#0f172a`): Deep carbon-charcoal for principal figures, metrics, and primary hierarchy.
  - `text-secondary` (`#475569`): Cool slate for column headers, metadata, and peripheral telemetry.
  - `text-tertiary` (`#94a3b8`): Low-emphasis units, timestamps, and trailing data indicators.

- **Functional & Accents:**
  - `primary` (`#2563eb`): Electric Cobalt for primary calls-to-action, focus rings, and active state highlights.
  - `secondary` (`#059669`): Emerald execution green for positive deltas, long exposure indicators, and affirmed statuses.
  - `critical` (`#dc2626`): Crimson for risk overruns, short signals, and stop events.
  - `tertiary` (`#0284c7`): Celestial cyan for secondary data streams, pending orders, and analytical overlays.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements three distinct typefaces to separate editorial structure, dense textual narrative, and computational telemetry.

- **Display & Headlines (Hanken Grotesk):** Provides razor-sharp geometric precision with subtle humanist warmth, preventing the daytime layout from feeling technical without elegance.
- **Narrative & UI Body (Geist):** Built for developer-centric density and legibility. Compact vertical bounds allow high-information presentation without line collision.
- **Metrics, Tickers, and Badges (JetBrains Mono):** Strict tabular figures for numerical columns, trade execution books, percentages, and timestamps to eliminate column jitter during real-time streaming updates.

### Rules of Usage
- All table data cells containing currency, timestamps, or quantitative measures must apply `mono-data-md` or `mono-data-sm`.
- Micro-labels, column indicators, and ticker identifiers must use `label-caps` in uppercase format.
- Avoid using weights higher than 600; high-contrast daytime UI relies on spatial contrast and hairline definition rather than typographic weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a compact, disciplined 4px grid system calibrated for multi-pane analytical dashboards, order books, and real-time execution panels.

### Structural Framework
- **Grid Structure:** Dynamic 12-column layout that shifts into a dense modular multi-pane split layout on displays wider than 1440px.
- **Gutter Strategy:** 
  - Standard displays: 1rem (16px) gutters.
  - Ultra-wide institutional workstations (≥ 1920px): 1.25rem (20px) gutters to preserve breathing room between continuous high-frequency modules.
- **Margins:** 
  - Mobile/Tablet: 1rem (16px) perimeter safety margin.
  - Desktop: 1.5rem (24px) perimeter margin hugging the outer chassis.

### Density & Rhythms
- **Element Tightness:** Default padding for metric panels and data lists utilizes `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (12px) to maximize above-the-fold telemetry density.
- **Reflow Logic:** Multi-column order books and market depth charts collapse sequentially from a side-by-side three-pane layout into a tabbed split-view below 1024px, preserving full horizontal span for numerical tables.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from dark-mode deep layering and heavy drop shadows. Depth is communicated via **luminous surface differentiation** and **structural zinc hairlines**.

### Surface Stratification
- **Floor (Canvas):** Tone `#f8fafc`. Acts as the inactive backplane for split gutters and overall window frames.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Data Tables):** Pure `#ffffff` surface with a strict `1px solid #e2e8f0` outer bounding stroke.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Modals):** Pure `#ffffff` surface, bounded by `1px solid #cbd5e1`, paired with a high-albedo ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Recessed Insets (Controls, Search, Input Cavities):** Tone `#f1f5f9` with an internal inset shadow: `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

### Optical Precision
- Avoid heavy, diffuse black shadows. Depth must feel like daylight cascading over machined titanium plates.
- Highlighting is achieved through sharp 1px borders rather than multi-layered elevation stacks.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is architectural and micro-radiused. Soft curves are restrained to 4px (`roundedness: 1`), reinforcing a crisp, engineered instrument feel.

### Geometric Hierarchy
- **Base Components (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Cells):** 0.25rem (4px) corner radius. Maintains clean horizontal and vertical line continuation when elements sit in tight proximity.
- **Containers & Module Panels:** 0.5rem (8px) corner radius (`rounded-lg`). Provides distinction between internal tools and outer system panels.
- **Floating Overlays & Modals:** 0.75rem (12px) corner radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Telemetry Badges & Pills:** Specialized full-pill radiuses are forbidden except for live connectivity status dots and streaming state badges, which utilize circular geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2563eb`, foreground `#ffffff`, border none. Hover: `#1d4ed8`. Active: `#1e40af`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#2563eb`. Font: `Geist` 13px weight 500. Height: 32px (dense) or 38px (standard).
- **Secondary / Panel Action:** Background `#ffffff`, foreground `#0f172a`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Hover: background `#f8fafc`, border `#cbd5e1`. Height: 32px.
- **Destructive:** Background `#ffffff`, foreground `#dc2626`, border `1px solid #fecaca`. Hover: background `#fef2f2`, border `#dc2626`.

### Data Badges & Telemetry Chips
- **Positive / Buy / Long:** Background `#ecfdf5`, border `1px solid #a7f3d0`, text `#059669`. Typography: `JetBrains Mono` 11px weight 500.
- **Negative / Sell / Short:** Background `#fef2f2`, border `1px solid #fecaca`, text `#dc2626`. Typography: `JetBrains Mono` 11px weight 500.
- **Neutral / Informational:** Background `#eff6ff`, border `1px solid #bfdbfe`, text `#2563eb`. Typography: `JetBrains Mono` 11px weight 500.
- Geometry: 4px border radius, vertical padding 2px, horizontal padding 6px.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`, placeholder text `#94a3b8`.
- Focus state: border-color `#2563eb`, outline `2px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- Density: 32px height for high-frequency input rows, with monospaced values for numerical trade volumes and pricing targets.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 14px × 14px square, radius 3px, border `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`. Checked: background `#2563eb`, border `#2563eb`, check icon pure white.
- **Segmented Switch Wells:** Background `#f1f5f9`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, internal active thumb `#ffffff` with hairline `1px solid #cbd5e1` and subtle lift `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.

### Data Grid / Tables
- Header: Background `#f8fafc`, text `#475569`, border-bottom `1px solid #e2e8f0`, typography `label-caps`. Height: 28px.
- Rows: Background `#ffffff`, alternate hover state `#f8fafc`, border-bottom `1px solid #f1f5f9`. Row height: 32px for maximum data visibility. Numbers right-aligned in `mono-data-md`.

### Cards & Execution Panels
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, border-radius 8px.
- Internal padding: `space-md` (12px) to `space-lg` (16px).
- Header bar: integrated divider line `1px solid #f1f5f9` with monospaced sub-label indicators.
